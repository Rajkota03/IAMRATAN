## Before anything: the collar problem

Every product photograph on your store shows a neck label reading **`LEGACY`** with a
mountain mark — not I Am Ratan. Your own brand document flags it. Meanwhile the buttons
appear to be engraved with your own name.

**Your shirts carry your name on the button and someone else's on the collar.** That is
your founding story as a physical object. It is also a live commercial risk: a customer
pays ₹6,999, opens the collar, sees a stranger's brand, and rationally reads counterfeit.

Every prompt below specifies **a blank neck label** for that reason.
