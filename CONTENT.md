## PAGE 1 — ABOUT US → maps to `house.html`

### HERO — full bleed image, minimal text

**I AM RATAN**
A brand made by Indians, for the world.

### OPENING SCENE

For twenty years, a room somewhere in India was full of hands that knew exactly
what they were doing.

They knew how a collar should stand before it's ever been worn. They knew the
half-degree difference between a shoulder that fits and one that almost does.
They built the garments that walked down the world's most photographed floors,
hung in the world's most expensive stores, sat inside the world's most recognised
boxes.

Not one of those boxes said who made them.

### THE TURN

That was the arrangement, and for a long time, it was enough. The craft
travelled. The credit didn't.

Then, somewhere between one collection and the next, a father and his daughter
who had spent their working lives building other people's names sat with a
simpler question than either of them expected to be asking after twenty years in
the trade:

> If we can build this for everyone else, why not for us?

There was no clean answer. There rarely is, when the question is really about
identity and not inventory. But there was a decision. And the decision had a name.

**I Am Ratan.**

Ratan is an old word. It means a jewel — not for its brilliance, but for its
rarity. Something discovered, refined, and held onto. Something too valuable to
disappear into the ordinary.

That is why we chose the name.

Not because it sounds beautiful, but because it defines the standard we refuse to
compromise on. We are not interested in making more. We are interested in making
fewer things, made better. Garments with the patience to be perfected, the
discipline to endure, and the character to be worn for years, not seasons.

Like the finest things ever built, true value does not ask to be noticed. It is
recognised. Quietly. Over time.

### WHAT CONTINUES

Nothing about the hands changed. The same discipline that once went into someone
else's label now goes into ours — design, pattern, cut, construction, all built
in-house, all held to the standard we spent two decades learning never to lower.

What changed is simpler than it sounds: the name on the inside of the collar is
finally the same as the name on the door.

I Am Ratan is premium Indian apparel — made with the discipline of a house that
has nothing left to prove to anyone but itself.

### THE ETHOS — kept as a quiet triad, not a marketing block

We call it Reclaim, Redefine, Resonate — not as a slogan, but because it's the
actual order things happened in.

- **Reclaim** — the authorship of work that was always ours.
- **Redefine** — what "premium" and "Indian" mean when said in the same sentence,
  without apology.
- **Resonate** — with people who'd rather wear who they are than wear who they're
  told to be.

### CLOSING

The truest expression of luxury has always been craftsmanship — not what is seen
first, but what remains long after the first impression fades.

It is built through patience, precision, and an unwavering respect for the craft
itself.

For years, our work spoke in someone else's name.
Today, it speaks in ours.

## PAGE 2 — BESPOKE → maps to `bespoke.html`

### HERO

**BESPOKE**

Some garments are made to fit a size.
Others are made to fit a person.

At I Am Ratan, every bespoke shirt begins not with a pattern, but with an
individual. Every measurement, every proportion, and every detail is considered
until the garment feels less like something worn and more like something that has
always belonged to you.

Because the difference between a good shirt and an exceptional one is measured in
millimetres and understood over a lifetime.

### OPENING — the idea before the offering

Most clothing is made for an average of you — an average shoulder, an average
wrist, an average idea of how you stand in a room. It's a reasonable compromise,
and most people never notice they're making it.

Bespoke, at I Am Ratan, is what happens when we stop averaging.

There are two ways into that world. One is about a single garment, built entirely
around one person. The other is about never running out of that certainty again.
Most of our clients eventually want both — but they rarely start there, so neither
will we.

### CHAPTER ONE — MADE TO MEASURE
*The garment that already knows you*

The first time we measure a client, we take over thirty individual measurements.
Not because thirty is an impressive number, but because no two people stand,
move, or carry themselves in quite the same way.

A shoulder sits a little lower. A posture shaped by years at a desk. A preferred
cuff that rests a fraction shorter than convention. Details so subtle they're
often ignored, yet together they define how a garment truly feels.

That understanding cannot be rushed.

Every commission begins with conversation — how you live, how you work, what has
never felt quite right, and what finally should. Only then do we guide you through
cloth, considering not just its appearance, but its weight, texture, drape, and
how it will accompany you through your days.

Only then does the work begin.

In our atelier, every pattern is cut for one individual alone. Every seam, every
collar, every sleeve is shaped with patience rather than haste. There are quicker
ways to make a shirt. We have simply never found one that preserved the integrity
of the craft.

What arrives in the end is more than a garment.

It is the sum of every detail no standard pattern could ever have known.

**CTA:** Book a consultation

### CHAPTER TWO — WARDROBE MANAGEMENT
*The decision you stop having to make*

There's a particular kind of relief in reaching for a shirt without thinking about
it — no second-guessing the fit, no mental note to reorder, no gap in the rotation
you'll only notice on the one morning you can't afford it.

Wardrobe Management exists to protect that feeling, permanently.

It starts the way Made to Measure does — with your fit and your fabric preferences
recorded once, properly, so they never need to be re-explained. From there, we do
something most bespoke houses won't: we look ahead. Based on how a wardrobe like
yours is actually used across a season, we build ten shirts to your exact
specification and hold them against the six months in front of you — not guessed
at, forecasted.

You commit to that cycle upfront, in full, before a single shirt is cut. It's not
a subscription and it isn't retail — it's a reservation of our atelier's time and
materials against your name, for a period, so that "I need a shirt" is never a
problem you have to solve on short notice again. When the six months end, the next
cycle is already waiting on the specification we already have. No re-measuring.
No re-deciding.

This is currently built around shirts. The same discipline is designed to extend
further, in time.

Wardrobe Management isn't about owning more shirts. It's about one fewer decision,
every single day, for six months at a time.

**CTA:** Enquire about wardrobe management services

### CLOSING

Every relationship with the house begins the same way: by understanding the person
before creating the garment.

Everything after that is simply craftsmanship, remembered.
