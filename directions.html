Photography drops straight in

Every image slot renders a drawn garment until a real file exists. Put a photograph at images/shirt-07.jpg and it replaces the drawing on all three variants at once. Nothing else changes. The full shot list and prompts are in IMAGE-BRIEF.md.

Contrast is measured, not guessed

Ink on all fifty colour fields is computed, not chosen by eye — near-black against bone, whichever clears 4.5:1. Storm Grey and Aegean Haze fall on opposite sides of that line. Cognac Drift clears neither, so it is the one field that opens to pure white.

No build step

Four HTML files and three scripts. Open any of them from disk, or drop the folder on any static host. Fonts are the only network request.
