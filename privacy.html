Privacy.

For the house

This page is a working draft so the site carries a complete trust layer. Values in [brackets] and anything that reads wrong should be corrected by the house before the site goes to customers.

What we collect. When you book a consultation or write to the house we receive what you give us: your name, your mobile number, your email address, and anything you choose to tell us. When an order is placed we also hold your measurements and your delivery address.

What it is used for

To speak with you, to make and deliver what you asked for, and to keep your measurements so the next shirt begins where the last one ended. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with anyone outside the house except the services that carry the work: payment, delivery, and the form service that brings your enquiry to us.

Where it lives

Enquiries reach us over [WhatsApp / Web3Forms] and are kept in the house’s records. We do not run advertising trackers. The site keeps a simple, anonymous count of visits that identifies no one.
